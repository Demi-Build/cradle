Dialogue editor & tester

Nine screens designing the editing and testing layer over cradle's read-only dialogue views. One recommended model: the graph grows editing affordances rather than being replaced, a navigator rail and ⌘P switcher handle characters with many trees, the tester is a bottom dock that lights the graph as it walks, and dialogue is authored at three scopes — NPC, quest, and scene. Real content throughout: Whisper-Tam and Rust-Kell from the 79-NPC sci-fi MazeWorld pack.

what changed in revision 2
Variants are replaced by selectors — a tree is chosen by an ordered predicate over any axis (quest state, segment, time, flag, room, scene attendance, player fields, or an axis you name), and the quest-gate becomes a router node whose row order is the precedence rule. The tester moved into a bottom dock. Two surfaces were added: quest-scoped dialogue across NPCs, and group scenes as their own entity.
