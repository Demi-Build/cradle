decision to make

Does the expression sheet generate with the states in step 4, or only when dialogue asks for it (P6)? Boarded here as one batch; the alternative saves money on characters that never speak.

what re-anchoring survives

Survives: id, lineage, skins, hand-edited frames (as versions), state list, mood enum. Flagged NR: every sheet generated against the old anchor. Nothing is deleted and nothing stops working — flagged sheets stay in the game until you replace them.
