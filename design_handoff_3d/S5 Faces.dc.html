why you are seeing neutral

A shape key named mood.weary exists but has no offsets — it was scaffolded, never shaped. Dialogue asking for weary will render neutral until it is authored, and the reason travels with the fallback.

how to author it
tier 2Shape-key scaffold + proportional ops

The agent can create the keys and apply measurable offsets — droop the brow 15%, close the lids 40%. Deterministic, free, judged as a before/after.

tier 3Sculpt it in Blender

A convincing weary face is a judgement about a character, not a percentage. Sculpt the key and come back through the round-trip.
