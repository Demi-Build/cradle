verdict · settled

Decided: ship the flat list; traversal is a filter, not a hierarchy.

Grouping earns its keep only where a key genuinely has more than one value — gravity in water vs on ground. That is three keys today. Making it the panel's spine hides eleven movement keys behind tabs and invents a taxonomy the pack schema does not have. A context chip on the keys that vary, plus the "following the player" auto-scroll from this board, gets the win without the tree.
