cradledoes the work
Stores each reference once, tags it by kind, and shows the composed prompt any surface will send. Curation is metadata; nothing is generated and nothing leaves the machine yet.
Runs each request with the board's on-references as guidance at their weights, and records which references steered each result — so the prompt-only enemy is identifiable later as the odd one out.
Multi-source img2img: the tray's images are the inputs, weights are the mix. The result is a normal entity whose lineage names its parts (spliced from scrap-hound, husk), and locking its anchor starts its own consistency chain.
Carries the boss's lineage into the SFX brief as material — the parts are audible suggestions, not decoration: a hound's grit plus a husk's hollow. Candidates play pinned to their frames in the running loop.

the thread that makes this work

Lineage is the through-line. Because the splice records its parts, step 4 and step 5 can ask "what is this thing made of?" and get a real answer. Nothing in this flow is retyped, and no screen needs a private copy of the references.

three strengths of influence
Curated — on a board, visible, changing nothing
Steering — in the prompt at a weight
Source — the image the result is generated from

Same library, three verbs. Confusing them is how tools end up steering with everything you ever uploaded.

to settle in build

Whether the image provider supports true multi-source img2img with weights, or whether a splice must be composed locally first and sent as one image. The design reads the same either way; the cost and quality do not.
