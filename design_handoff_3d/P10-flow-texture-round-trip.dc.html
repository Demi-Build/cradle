Painting a texture is a PNG job, so PixEd does it — with the palette roles and QA chips it already enforces on sprites. Unwrapping is not a PixEd feature; it is one tier-2 operation that happens first. The payoff is at the end: renders pick the texture up, so conditioning images finally carry the character's colours.

why PixEd and not a 3D paint tool

Projection painting on a mesh is Blender's territory and a poor use of our build. A texture atlas is a PNG with regions, and PixEd's palette discipline and QA are exactly what keeps a hand-painted atlas consistent with the sprite sheets it will condition.
