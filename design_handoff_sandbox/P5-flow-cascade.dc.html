cradledoes the work
Writes the new anchor version and walks the provenance graph for artifacts conditioned on the old one. The walk is free and instant — it is metadata, not pixels.
Marks each dependent artifact NR — needs review — and leaves it fully usable. Validation chips on affected levels clear, per house rule: no stale “valid ✓”. Nothing regenerates on its own.
Builds the plan in Phase 1's card language: ordered steps, each with its verb, its inputs, its estimate, and what it will overwrite. Hand-edited artifacts are separated out, because those are the ones a rerun destroys.
Runs one step per confirm, writing results as they land and updating the rollup with actual spend beside the estimate. Skipped steps stay in the card as skipped, not removed.
Re-runs the free validator and QA checks on what changed, and keeps the paused plan attached to the entity's History so the remaining steps are one click away — not a thing to remember.

failure · mid-plan

Step 3 of 4 fails: the card marks that step failed with the provider's message, keeps steps 1–2's results, and offers retry on the failed step alone. Actual spend to date is shown next to the estimate.

the hard case

Regenerating over a hand-edited frame is the only destructive act in the flow. It is a separate confirm with the frame named and a thumbnail: “This replaces your edit to jump · frame 3. The edit stays in History.”

reuses

The agent panel's plan card, its per-step confirm and cost line, and the History/lineage tab. The only new part is the blast-radius rollup header.
