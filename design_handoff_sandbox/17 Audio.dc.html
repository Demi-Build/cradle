Audio · music against the level, SFX against the frames

Two timelines, because audio has two clocks. Music runs along the level — a spatial timeline you scrub by walking. SFX run along a state's frames — a footstep belongs to frame 2, not to a second. Both live in the dock, never in a modal: you cannot judge either one without the thing playing.

Emitters placed on the level like anything else: a point with a radius and falloff, or a region that fades in as you enter. Placed with the sandbox's Add ▸ Sound, tuned here or in Adjust.

why two timelines, not one

Music is spatial: it changes because the player moved, so its axis is cells and its objects are the level's music zones. SFX is per-state: a footstep belongs to a frame, so its axis is the filmstrip. Forcing both onto seconds would desync every sound the moment fps or run speed changed.

where it opens from
music zone in the level editor → Music pane, that region selected
a state in the Animation tab or character editor → SFX pane, that state
a dialogue line → SFX pane, scoped to the line
⌘K or the agent → the same pane, staged
empty and failure states

No track on a region: the lane reads no track · silence and silence is a valid answer, not an error. No audio device: the timelines still edit, the play button is disabled with the reason. A generation leg down keeps what landed and re-prices the rest, as everywhere else.
