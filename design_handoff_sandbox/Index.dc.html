Sandbox Stages

Twelve surfaces and seven processes, split into the two halves of the product: the editors, where content is designed and generated, and the sandbox, where it is tuned and played. Every board is drawn at 1440 with the agent panel where it actually sits, in both themes across the set. Building from this? Start at HANDOFF.md. The interaction spec is in README.md; the implementation map in PLAN.md.
